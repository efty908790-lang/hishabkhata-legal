Delete your HishabKhata account

You can ask us to delete your HishabKhata account and all of your online backups. This cannot be undone.

How to request deletion
  1. Send an e-mail to shayan.ahmed.arish@gmail.com from the e-mail address of your account, with the subject "Delete my HishabKhata account".
  2. We delete your account within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted: your account, your online backup (parties, entries, cash, stock, notes), your sign-in sessions and your payment records on our server, and any backup copy in the owner's Google Drive.

What is not deleted: the data on your phone. Remove it in the app (Settings > Delete all data) or by uninstalling the app.

See also the Privacy Policy.
