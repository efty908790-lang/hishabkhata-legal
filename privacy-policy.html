HishabKhata Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

HishabKhata is a khata (ledger) app for shopkeepers. It works offline. This page explains what is stored and how to remove it.

What is stored on your phone

Customers and suppliers, entries, cash, stock, notes, reminders and settings are saved on your phone. The App lock PIN is saved on your phone only (as a salted hash) and is never sent to us.

What we store online (only if you sign up / sign in)

  • Your name, e-mail address and Google account ID (or a password hash if you set a password).
  • Your online backup: the ledger data you keep in the app (parties and their phone numbers, entries, cash, stock, notes).
  • If you buy a plan: the plan, amount, payment method and the bKash / Nagad transaction ID you enter.
  • Temporary technical data to stop abuse (for example, the number of recent sign-in tries).

A copy of each backup may also be kept in the app owner's private Google Drive for safekeeping. Data is sent over HTTPS.

What we do not do

No ads, no tracking, no analytics. We do not sell or share your data. The "Call" and "Send reminder message" buttons only open your phone's dialer or messaging app; the app never sends messages by itself.

Services used

Google Sign-In (to sign in with your Google account) and, optionally, Google Drive (owner's backup copy).

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. Deleting removes your account, online backups, sign-in sessions and payment records from our server (a copy in the owner's Google Drive is removed on request).

Delete your data

See the delete-account page. Data on your phone is removed with Settings > Delete all data, or by uninstalling the app.

Children

The app is not meant for children under 13.
